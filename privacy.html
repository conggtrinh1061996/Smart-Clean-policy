Privacy Policy for Smart Cleaner

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Smart Cleaner ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our application collects, uses, and safeguards your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information Collection and Device Permissions

To perform its core device optimization and cleaning features, Smart Cleaner requires access to certain permissions on your device:

  • Post Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used to deliver timely alerts regarding junk file buildup, memory status, and scheduled maintenance notifications.
  • Read & Write External Storage (READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): Used to scan for temporary files, cache, duplicate items, and residual files, allowing you to free up storage space.
  • Read Photos & Videos (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / READ_MEDIA_VIDEO): Used strictly to locate large media files, duplicate photos, or videos that you may choose to clean up or organize.

Note: All file scanning and cleaning operations take place entirely on your device locally. We do not transfer, upload, or store your photos, videos, or personal files on external servers.

2. Data Usage and Third-Party Services

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal data or device contents to third parties. Non-personal device analytics may be used solely for crash reporting and app performance improvement.

3. Data Security

We implement security measures to protect your local data access. Since user data remains on your physical device, maintaining local security relies primarily on your device's security settings.

4. Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
